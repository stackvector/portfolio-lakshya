# 13 — Visual Direction

Based on the reference, but adapted to your existing preferences:

### Overall feeling

**Premium + quiet + modern + technical**

Not:

* neon cyberpunk
* terminal aesthetic
* excessive gradients
* "AI startup" cliché
* huge collection of floating cards
* overly colorful dashboard

### Typography

Use:

* very large editorial hero typography
* small uppercase section labels
* restrained body text
* strong hierarchy between headings and supporting text

### Layout

Use:

* generous whitespace
* strong horizontal sections
* thin borders/dividers
* asymmetric layouts where appropriate
* large project titles
* compact metadata
* subtle hover interactions

### Cards

Don't make every section a giant rounded card.

Prefer:

```text
──────────────
PROJECT

Title

Problem
...

Approach
...

Outcome
...

React · Next.js · Supabase
──────────────
```

This is closer to the **editorial/product-engineering feel** of the reference.

### Motion

Keep it subtle:

* section reveal
* text movement
* hover transitions
* slight card/image movement
* smooth navigation

No excessive particle effects.
